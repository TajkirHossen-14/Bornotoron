যেভাবে বলেন, সেভাবেই লিখুন। Uppercase matters: o → অ, O → ও. After a consonant, o uses its inherent vowel; O adds ো.

Try kh → খ, sundor → সুন্দর, kO → কো. c → চ, ch / chh → ছ. Everyday spellings are helped by a built-in dictionary. Banglish is flexible; click any output word to make it yours.
